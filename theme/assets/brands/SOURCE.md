# Manufacturer marks

Manufacturer marks use official-color transparent variants where the company
publishes a color master: Nintendo red, SEGA blue, Microsoft's four-color
window, Atari red, Commodore blue, NEC red, and Apple gray. Sony remains a
white knockout because its corporate wordmark has no distinctive multicolor
master. They are rasterized locally for Qt 5.

The Nintendo mark is rendered directly from the official vector-derived SVG.
Its PNG uses `#e60012` as the sole nontransparent RGB color and stores edge
antialiasing only in alpha, preventing white-matte fringe on dark backgrounds.
