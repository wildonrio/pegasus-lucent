# Manufacturer marks

Manufacturer marks use official-color transparent variants where the company
publishes a color master: Nintendo red, SEGA blue, and Microsoft's four-color
window. Sony remains a white knockout because its corporate wordmark has no
distinctive multicolor master. They are rasterized locally for Qt 5.

The Nintendo mark is rendered directly from the official vector-derived SVG.
Its PNG uses `#e60012` as the sole nontransparent RGB color and stores edge
antialiasing only in alpha, preventing white-matte fringe on dark backgrounds.

Arcade is intentionally labeled `MULTI-PUBLISHER` and has no manufacturer mark.
